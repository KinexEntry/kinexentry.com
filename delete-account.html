Delete your account

You can have your Kinex Entry account and the personal information on it deleted at any time. This applies to the Kinex Entry app (Android, package com.kinex.entry) and to office console accounts.

How to request it

  1. Email info@kinexentry.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my Kinex Entry account".
  2. We confirm the request by replying to that address, so nobody can delete an account that isn't theirs.
  3. The account is deleted within 30 days of confirmation. You'll get one email when it's done.
If you have an entry in a rodeo that hasn't happened yet, withdraw first, or tell us in the email and we'll withdraw it for you under that rodeo's refund rule.

What is deleted

  • Your login, name, email, phone, address, hometown and date of birth
  • Association cards, horses and documents on your profile
  • Your profile photo
  • Watchlists and waiting-list positions

What is kept, and why

  • Published results for rodeos you competed in — name, hometown, times, scores, placings and payouts. These are the rodeo's official record, held for the rodeo, and stay with your name as the rodeo published them.
  • Payment records — what was paid, when, for which entry — for as long as tax and accounting law require.

Nothing else is retained. Deletion is permanent; a new account starts empty.

Deleting only some things

You don't need to delete the account to remove a document, a horse, a card or your photo — each can be removed in the app under Profile at any time.
