Contestants

Everything You Need In One App

  • Browse events by association or open events
  • Enter in seconds, pay fees by card, see who all is entered, check event details and notes about the event
  • Check results and payouts for all events listed, at no additional cost to the contestant
  • List all your association cards, horses, Coggins and accolades
  • See where you land in the run order of the event
  • Draw out from the app and upload doctor or vet forms straight to the secretary
  • Books open for event notifications
  • After hours entry through AI agent (coming soon)
Producers / Associations

List an event, and let the entries come to you

  • Submit your event — logo, performances and slack, events, caps, fees, sanctions, payout rules
  • List if you want entry fees taken online, at the event, or both options for contestants
  • Set your number of contestants that run in the performance, the rest go to slack or on a waiting list
  • Allow card holder first preference with one click of a box
  • No more manning the phones for call ins. Our office handles those and after hours is handled by our AI agent that never sleeps and is available 24/7
Secretaries & timers

The books, the draw, the day sheet, the money

  • The draw, per class or the whole event, seeded so it can be rerun and proven
  • Printable day sheets with names, hometowns, stock numbers and names
  • Results entry with in-pot and out-of-pot handled correctly
  • Payouts computed from the event's own rules
  • A personnel directory with cards and contact details
Announcers

The day sheet, ready before you are

  • Print performance day sheets with the draw listed
  • Homework is done for you: the contestants list their accolades and they print directly on the day sheets
  • Print results from earlier performances
  • Contestant hometowns and horse's name, if applicable, printed right on the day sheet
Video Screen Companies

Every name on the screen, none of them typed twice

  • Print event day sheets with contestant names, hometowns, and accolades
  • No more having to enter each contestant. Download a CSV file of the day sheets and upload it directly into the Kinex Judging System (visit kinexjudging.com for more information)
  • See results from previous events for contestant graphic cards
  • List your company on the directory to be found by producers and associations
