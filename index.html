Rodeo entry

Enter up, check your draw, and get paid — from your phone.

Kinex Entry is how contestants enter rodeos and how the office runs them. One account, every rodeo you enter, the draw and the results the moment the secretary posts them.

Contestants

Everything you need before you leave the truck

  • Browse rodeos by association — IPRA, WPRA and open events — with fees and check-in times up front
  • Enter in seconds and pay fees in the app
  • Your draw: position, stock, performance time
  • Results and payouts as they're recorded
  • Cards, horses and Coggins dates on your profile so every entry is eligible the first time
  • Withdraw with a doctor's or vet's release, straight from your phone
Producers

List an event, and let the entries come to you

  • Submit your rodeo — logo, performances and slack, events, caps, fees, sanctions, payout rules — for listing
  • Keep your stock on file and tie it to the rodeo
  • See exactly why a listing was sent back, and fix it
  • Waiting lists that fill themselves
Secretaries & timers

The books, the draw, the day sheet, the money

  • The draw, per class or the whole rodeo, seeded so it can be rerun and proven
  • Printable day sheets with names, hometowns, stock numbers and names
  • Results entry with in-pot and out-of-pot handled correctly
  • Payouts computed from the rodeo's own rules
  • A personnel directory with cards and contact details

Working a rodeo?

Producers, secretaries and timers use the office console on the web. Access is approved by hand: make an account, tell us which outfit you work for and which association cards you hold, and we verify them before you're let in.
