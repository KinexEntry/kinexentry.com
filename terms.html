1. What the service is

Kinex Entry takes rodeo entries from contestants and hands them to the rodeo that is running the event. The rodeo — its producer, secretary and committee — sets the rules: who is eligible, the fees, the draw, the results, the payout and any refunds. Kinex Entry records and carries out those rules; it does not make them. Each rodeo's rules are shown on its listing before you enter.

3. Entries, fees and refunds

  • An entry is a commitment to the rodeo. Fees are set by the rodeo and shown before you confirm.

  • Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider under their terms. Kinex Entry does not store card details.

4. The draw, results and payouts

The draw is produced by the rodeo office using the service, from a recorded seed, so it can be rerun and shown to be fair. Results and payouts are entered by the rodeo's secretary and are the rodeo's official record. Disputes about a draw, a time, a score or a payout are resolved by the rodeo under its own and its sanctioning association's rules, not by Kinex Entry.
